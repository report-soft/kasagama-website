Welcome to Kasagama Secondary School

Our History & Growth

Founded as a community school in 2003 through parent contributions, Kasagama Secondary School was officially acquired by the Government in 2017. In 2025, the school moved to its modern new campus next to Kasagama Anglican Church, fully equipped with state-of-the-art furniture, computers, books, science laboratories, and projectors.

Headteacher's Message

Dear All,

I welcome you to Kasagama Secondary School website. We are an Anglican founded, government aided day and boarding secondary school. Our staff is comitted to nurturing students into responsible and self-reliant citizens.


Guided by our school motto, Education for Prosperity, we strive to provide quality, affordable education that equips learners with knowledge and skills, turning them into productive members of society.

Administration

The school is governed by the Board of Governors. Day-to-Day running of the school is headed by the Headteacher (Ssenfuma Herbert Mitti). The headteacher is deputized by the Deputy Headteacher (Nakazzi Manjeri), and assisted by the Deputy Headteacher Academics, Director of Studies (DOS), and the Assistant Director of Studies.

Admissions Procedure

Kasagama Secondary School admits students joining S1 – S3 and S5. Admissions are processed by the Headteacher and Deputy Headteacher.

Senior One (S1)

  • PLE Recommendation Letter
  • 3 Passport Photographs
  • Primary School LIN
  • Admission Fees

Senior Two & Three (S2–S3)

  • PLE Result Slip
  • Most Recent Report Card
  • Former School LIN
  • 3 Passport Photos & Fees

Senior Five (S5)

  • UCE Result Slip
  • Former School LIN
  • 3 Passport Photographs
  • Admission Fees
